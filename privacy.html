Privacy notice: international debating enquiry form

Cognasium India · last updated 19 November 2026

What the form collects

  • Who is filling it in (parent or guardian, or other)
  • Your child's grade and, if you choose one, their debating level. We do not ask for your child's name or any other details about your child.
  • Your full name, mobile number, email address and, if you give it, your city
  • The page address and the advert tags in the link you came from (for example utm_source), so we know which advert you saw

Who receives it

Your enquiry is emailed to the Cognasium India team. It is sent through our email provider (Resend). Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the form is filled in by a person and not a bot.

To stop the same enquiry being sent twice, a scrambled (hashed) copy of your mobile number and email address is kept for up to 24 hours. It cannot be read back as your number or email.

This page uses the Meta Pixel to tell Meta that an enquiry was sent from the advert. Your name, mobile number and email address are not sent to Meta.

How we use it

Only to contact you about the Cognasium India international debating programme. We do not sell it, share it for marketing or use it for anything else.

Deleting your details

Email cognasiumindia@gmail.com and ask us to delete your enquiry. We will delete it and confirm by email.
